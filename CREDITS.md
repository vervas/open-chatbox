# Chatbox web demo — credits

## Music

All six tracks are by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
downloaded from Wikimedia Commons.

| Demo album | Track | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Silly Songs | Monkeys Spinning Monkeys | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Monkeys_Spinning_Monkeys_(ISRC_USUAN1400011).mp3 |
| Silly Songs | Fluffing a Duck | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fluffing_a_Duck_(ISRC_USUAN1100768).mp3 |
| Sunny Afternoon | Carefree | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carefree_(ISRC_USUAN1400037).mp3 |
| Sunny Afternoon | Local Forecast | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Local_Forecast_(ISRC_USUAN1300010).mp3 |

| Sleepy Time | Gymnopédie No. 2 (Erik Satie, performed by Kevin MacLeod) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kevin_MacLeod_-_Erik_Satie_Gymnopedie_No_2.ogg |

Changes: each track is cut to a 36-second excerpt from the start with a
fade in/out, re-encoded as 80 kbit/s MP3 with new tags, and grouped into
three albums invented for this demo ("Silly Songs", "Sunny Afternoon",
"Sleepy Time"). They are not real Kevin MacLeod albums.

## Art

- Album covers (`pi/web/music/**/cover.png`) and faces (`pi/web/faces/`):
  drawn from shapes by `pi/web/make_art.py` for this demo. Cover titles use
  M PLUS Rounded 1c (SIL Open Font License, `pi/fonts/`).
- Emoji: the kiosk's own set, `pi/src/assets/emoji/`.

## Software

LVGL 9.2 (MIT), SQLite (public domain), SDL2 (zlib), Emscripten (MIT /
University of Illinois NCSA). Fonts: Didact Gothic and M PLUS Rounded 1c
(SIL OFL), DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera licence).

## Pretend

The contacts, conversations and replies are made up. Nothing typed in the
demo leaves the page.
